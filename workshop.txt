title=Infection Diagnosis
description=Adds a Microscope Slide item to the game which you can use to gather a blood sample and inspect it at a microscope to check for KNOX infection.
description=
description=Microscope Slide item has been added to TestingLab, ScienceMisc and Chemistry loot tables and will spawn at locations such as labs.
description=
description=You need to have a Needle or Sharp Knife in your inventory and First Aid level 1 for the context menu option Gather Blood Sample to show. The option will be red if the sample has already been inspected.
description=
description=If you have a Microscope Slide(Blood Sample) in your inventory you can right click a microscope in the world to get the Inspect Sample option.
description=
description=The results of the inspection will show on the Microscope Slide tooltip.
description=
description=
